# LearnEnglish 儿童隐私政策 / Children's Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective Date: 2026-06-02

---

## 中文版

本《儿童隐私政策》是对《LearnEnglish 隐私政策》的补充,专门面向监护人说明我们如何保护 3-5 岁儿童的在线隐私。本政策与《隐私政策》同时适用;如有冲突,以保护儿童更严格者为准。

### 1. 本应用的目标用户

LearnEnglish 专为 3-5 岁儿童设计,提供英语启蒙学习内容。所有面向儿童的内容界面均使用简单、友好、无诱导性的语言。

### 2. 我们收集的儿童信息(最小化)

2.1 我们**仅**收集实现本应用核心功能所必需的儿童信息:

- **学习进度数据**(本地存储):每个字母 / 单词 / 短句的练习次数、最近一次学习日期、是否已掌握
- **家长控制设置**(由监护人设置):每日学习时长上限、声音/语言偏好
- **临时语音录音**(仅在监护人授予麦克风权限后):用于实时发音评分,评分后立即从内存销毁,不写入文件、不上传

2.2 我们**不会**收集以下儿童信息:

- 真实姓名、昵称、邮箱、手机号、地址、学校等任何可识别个人身份的信息
- 真实照片、视频、地理位置、通讯录、健康数据
- 家庭成员信息
- 任何形式的生物识别信息(指纹、面部等)
- 任何形式的广告标识符(IDFA 等)

2.3 我们**不会**要求儿童提供任何形式的账号注册、密码、或可识别个人身份的信息。

### 3. 可验证的家长同意(Verifiable Parental Consent)

3.1 本应用通过 **Parental Gate** 机制在涉及儿童的关键操作前获取监护人验证。Parental Gate 采用面向成人的数学题(如"7 + 5 = ?")、连续 3 次错误后锁定 5 分钟等措施,确保操作由成人完成。

3.2 需要通过 Parental Gate 的操作包括但不限于:
- 进入「家长中心」
- 调整家长控制(每日时长上限、声音偏好、推送开关等)
- 发起订阅购买
- 重置学习进度
- 跳转到外部链接(隐私政策、用户协议、订阅条款等)

3.3 我们不在 Parental Gate 中使用儿童可能轻易猜出的验证方式(如下滑解锁、点击特定颜色等)。

### 4. 数据存储与传输

4.1 **本地优先**:全部学习数据存储在儿童所使用的 iOS 设备本地(通过 UserDefaults 与 FileManager)。我们不运营任何后端服务器。

4.2 **不向云端上传儿童个人信息**:除 Apple 要求的订阅收据验证(由 Apple 直接处理,不经过我们的服务器)外,本应用不向任何云端传输儿童信息。

4.3 **录音不持久化**:语音录音仅存在于内存,评分完成后立即销毁,不会写入本地文件、不会上传到云端。

### 5. 第三方披露

我们**不会**与任何第三方共享、出售、出租儿童个人信息。我们不接入任何第三方广告 SDK、第三方分析 SDK、第三方社交 SDK。

### 6. 家长权利

监护人享有以下针对其儿童信息的权利:

- **查看**:在应用内「设置 → 学习进度」可查看孩子的学习数据
- **删除**:在应用内「设置 → 重置学习进度」可一键清除所有本地数据;卸载本应用同样可以达到完全删除
- **撤回授权**:在 iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → LearnEnglish」中可随时关闭麦克风、删除本地数据
- **停止使用**:卸载本应用即可停止本应用的全部数据收集

### 7. 内容安全

7.1 面向儿童的内容在发布前经过人工审核,确保:
- 无暴力、血腥、恐怖元素
- 无成人话题(恋爱、酒精、烟草、毒品、政治等)
- 无可诱导消费的语言(如"再不学就落后了"等焦虑营销话术)
- 无外部链接、广告、跳转

7.2 应用内购买必须通过 Parental Gate,且由 Apple 负责支付环节的合规校验。

### 8. 政策更新与通知

8.1 我们可能根据法律变更或产品迭代更新本政策。更新后会在 GitHub `md-doc` 仓库发布。

8.2 **重大变更会通过应用内通知告知监护人**,并在监护人下次启动应用时要求重新确认。

8.3 我们不会在未通知监护人的情况下实质性扩大对儿童信息的收集范围。

### 9. 联系我们

如监护人对本政策或本应用处理儿童信息的方式有任何疑问、意见或投诉,请通过以下方式联系我们,我们将在 15 个工作日内回复:

- 开发者:wangzz
- 邮箱:support@learnenglish.example.com
- 项目主页:https://github.com/wangzz/LearnEnglish

如您认为我们对儿童信息的处理未能充分保护儿童权益,您也可以向您所在国家 / 地区的数据保护监管机构投诉。

## English Version

This Children's Privacy Policy supplements the [LearnEnglish Privacy Policy](https://github.com/wangzz/md-doc/blob/main/LearnEnglish/PRIVACY.md) and is provided specifically to inform Guardians of how we protect the online privacy of children aged 3-5. This policy and the Privacy Policy apply concurrently; in case of conflict, the more protective standard prevails.

### 1. Audience of the App

LearnEnglish is designed for children aged 3-5 to introduce English. All child-facing content uses simple, friendly, non-persuasive language.

### 2. Children's Information We Collect (Minimized)

2.1 We collect **only** the information necessary for the App's core functionality:

- **Learning progress data** (local storage): practice counts per letter / word / sentence, last-practice date, mastery state
- **Parental control settings** (set by the Guardian): daily time limits, audio / language preferences
- **Transient voice recordings** (only after the Guardian grants microphone permission): used for real-time pronunciation scoring, destroyed from memory immediately after scoring — never written to file, never uploaded

2.2 We do **not** collect:

- Real name, nickname, email, phone, address, school, or any personally identifiable information
- Real photos, videos, location, contacts, health data
- Family member information
- Biometric data (fingerprint, face, etc.)
- Any form of advertising identifier (IDFA, etc.)

2.3 We do **not** require children to register accounts, set passwords, or provide personally identifiable information.

### 3. Verifiable Parental Consent (VPC)

3.1 The App uses a **Parental Gate** to verify the Guardian before operations that affect children. The Parental Gate uses an adult-oriented math problem (e.g., "7 + 5 = ?") and locks for 5 minutes after 3 consecutive wrong answers.

3.2 Operations requiring the Parental Gate include, but are not limited to:
- Entering the Parent Center
- Adjusting parent controls (daily time limits, audio preferences, push toggles, etc.)
- Initiating a subscription purchase
- Resetting learning progress
- Opening external links (Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Subscription Terms, etc.)

3.3 We do not use verification methods a child could easily guess (e.g., swipe-to-unlock, tap a specific color).

### 4. Storage and Transmission

4.1 **Local-first**: All learning data is stored locally on the child's iOS device (via UserDefaults and FileManager). We operate no backend server.

4.2 **No children's personal information is uploaded to the cloud**: except for Apple-required subscription receipt validation (handled by Apple directly, not by our servers), the App does not transmit any children's information to any cloud service.

4.3 **Recordings are not persisted**: voice recordings exist only in memory and are destroyed immediately after scoring. They are not written to local files and not uploaded.

### 5. Third-Party Disclosure

We do **not** share, sell, or rent children's personal information with any third party. We do not integrate any third-party advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or social SDK.

### 6. Parental Rights

The Guardian has the following rights regarding the child's information:

- **View**: see the child's learning data in-app under Settings → Learning Progress
- **Delete**: tap Settings → Reset Learning Progress to clear all local data; uninstalling the App achieves the same result
- **Withdraw authorization**: at any time go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → LearnEnglish to revoke microphone access or delete local data
- **Stop using**: uninstalling the App halts all data collection by the App

### 7. Content Safety

7.1 Child-facing content is human-reviewed before release to ensure:
- No violence, gore, or horror
- No adult topics (romance, alcohol, tobacco, drugs, politics, etc.)
- No persuasive language that could induce consumption (e.g., "fall behind if you don't learn")
- No external links, ads, or redirects

7.2 In-app purchases must pass the Parental Gate; payment compliance is handled by Apple.

### 8. Updates and Notice

8.1 We may update this policy as required by law or product evolution. Updated versions are published in the GitHub `md-doc` repository.

8.2 **Material changes will be communicated to Guardians via in-app notice** and require reconfirmation on the Guardian's next launch of the App.

8.3 We will not materially expand the scope of children's information collection without notifying the Guardian.

### 9. Contact Us

If the Guardian has any questions, comments, or complaints about this policy or how we handle children's information, please contact us; we will respond within 15 business days:

- Developer: wangzz
- Email: support@learnenglish.example.com
- Project: https://github.com/wangzz/LearnEnglish

If you believe our handling of children's information is insufficient, you may also file a complaint with the data protection authority in your country / region.
